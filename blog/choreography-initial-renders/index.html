Choreography Engine: Initial Renders

The first batch of renders from the choreography engine.

By Fotoh Mbaku · 8 videos
  • Single moves: built from templates. A short spec fills in an aerial strike or a ground combo.
  • Fights: built by the new fight pipeline from a list of beats (attacks, answers, arts, footwork). Both fighters are solved in one Blender scene, with the blades meeting by IK, then directed and rendered with synthesised sound.
  • All are rendered in the autumn courtyard. The videos have sound.

Descending Heron

Single move, built from the aerial strike template. A two-step run, a forward somersault over the foe, a pause at the top, then one downward cut that cracks the ground on landing.

Crossing Rain

Single move, built from the ground combo template. Four cuts with a step in on each: right to left, left to right, rising and falling. Each cut is picked from the clip catalogue by the direction the blade travels.

First Frost

Quinn Ashe vs the Hollow Swordsman. A straight sword exchange of fifteen beats: blocks, parries, dodges and two blade clashes.

Seven Slashes of the Gale

The Hollow Swordsman vs Quinn Ashe. A short exchange, then Wandering Crescent spliced in whole. Quinn blocks the first slash and takes the rest, ending in a knockdown.

Falling Leaves

The Hollow Swordsman vs Quinn Ashe. Nine exchanges, then the Falling Leaves art. Quinn raises a high guard and is knocked down by the impact.

Winter's Edge

Quinn Ashe vs the Hollow Swordsman. Quinn uses the engine's two new moves in a fight: Crossing Rain, then Descending Heron for the knockdown.

The Severing

Quinn Ashe vs the Hollow Swordsman. Thirteen exchanges, ending with a killing backhand from Quinn.

Crossfire

The Hollow Swordsman vs Quinn Ashe at range. It opens with Falling Leaves, then both throw ki waves across the yard. Most are dodged, and two waves meet in mid-air.
