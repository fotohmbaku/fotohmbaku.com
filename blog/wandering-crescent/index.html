Wandering Crescent

The Hollow Swordsman's signature attack, made as a cinematic and as a game skill from one Blender animation.

By Fotoh Mbaku · 2 min read

Animation

  • Built from the game's own motion-capture clips, dumped from Godot and converted into Blender exactly.
  • Clips are blended in timed segments, with hand IK on the hilt and scabbard, planted feet, and a landing aimed so the blade hits the target.
  • Cape, sash, skirt and hair are extra chain bones, baked as verlet ropes in wind.
  • A timeline file records every event and per-frame sample, shared by the effects, the cameras and the game export.

Choreography

  • Frames 0–50: low iai stance while the aura gathers.
  • 50–64: slash 1, a rising draw.
  • 64–120: slashes 2–4 as three blinks round the target, slash 5 a rising cut, slash 6 a twist in the air.
  • 120–170: somersault and plunge. Slash 7 strikes the ground: rings, cracks, debris and a shockwave.
  • 170–330: look back and sheathe.

Rendering

  • Toon materials, an ink outline, and Kuwahara brushwork over paper grain in the compositor.
  • Eleven shots, each with its own camera and time map for slow motion.
  • All effects are rebuilt per frame by script: trail, swirl, after-images, ribbons, rings, cracks, rocks and the title.
  • The soundtrack is synthesised by a Python script.

In game

  • The skill galeSeven is the first skill of the Acrobatic Swordsmanship manual.
  • The Blender animation is exported back to the game skeleton (round-trip error about 3 microns) and packed as a glTF clip.
  • Nine beats slice the clip, with blinks to set positions round the foe, a homing plunge, and aerial beats that hold height.
  • Covered by an automated test in the Godot suite.
